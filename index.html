The guideLitres, not inches

Outside dimensions tell you whether a bag will slide into a sizer, and almost nothing about whether four days of clothes will go in it. Volume is the number that survives.

Every bag is filled with sealed one-litre bags of rice, packed the way an ordinary person packs, the lid closed without force, and the count rounded down to the whole litre. To size a bag you own, multiply the inside length, width and depth in centimetres and divide by one thousand.

Five to eight litres is pocket contents plus one object. Nine to twelve takes a tablet flat. Fourteen to twenty-two is a working day. Above ninety you are moving, not travelling.

How it worksOrdering, delivery and returns

The trolley is a list held in your own browser; nothing travels while you browse. The order form writes it out as plain text, one line per bag, and FormSubmit forwards it with your name, email and address to our mailbox. No account, nothing parked on a server.

A person answers by email, usually inside one working day and never past the third, confirming stock, pricing delivery for your postcode and giving a total. Nothing is charged before that.

We ship within the United States only, by courier, to a street address. Fourteen days from arrival to send a bag back unused and tagged. We do not repair, fit, personalise or offer credit.
